## Inspiration

This is a repaint of my bicycle, Lessons. Lessons is aptly named as it is a vehicle for learning. Not just how to dimple chain stays or use and airbrush, but also patience and perseverance. When you are trying new things and pushing your limits, you have to learn to give yourself grace and pe as patient with yourself as you are with your work.
