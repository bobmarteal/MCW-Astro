## Inspiration

This one was for my son. He wanted something sharp — clean lines, a color palette pulled from the world of performance cars. Porsche Blue was the anchor, that deep, confident hue that looks different in every light. From there the palette came together around it naturally. The Giant frame was a great canvas that allowed for a stunner of a road bike.
