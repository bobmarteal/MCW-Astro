## Inspiration

This project was for a very dear friend. They completely trusted me to take a perfectly good Specialized Tri-Cross, strip it down to bare aluminum and carbon fiber, and create something unique that would instil joy in those that saw it. The design was just as challenging as the painting. I hope it continues to bring smiles for untold miles and miles.

## Design brief

> I want a bike that is colorful and brings me joy
> ... and I would like birds on it.
