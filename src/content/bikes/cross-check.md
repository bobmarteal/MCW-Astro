## Inspiration

This is the bike that got me into all of this. I was wrenching on it one afternoon when I sheared off a bolt, and instead of panicking, I cut new threads. That moment of realizing *oh, you can just cut metal* unlocked something. If you can cut metal, you can reshape it. If you can reshape it, you can build with it.

The Cross-Check already had good bones. I wanted to repaint it properly — not a rattle-can refresh, but a real paint job that made it a genuine 1-of-1. A Surly no one else has. That project led to the next one, and the next one, and here we are.
