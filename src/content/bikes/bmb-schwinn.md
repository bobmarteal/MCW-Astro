## Inspiration

[Bike Monday Bros](https://www.instagram.com/bikemondaybros/) is a weekly ride that has grown into something much bigger — a genuine community of generous, kind people who show up every Monday for good conversation and good pedaling. I can't say enough good things about them.

One member mentioned he was restoring one of his dad's old Schwinns and wondered if I could help tidy it up. One Monday ride later, the idea had grown into something better: repaint it properly and make it a loaner — a bike anyone could borrow if they wanted to come out but didn't have a ride. That's just how this crew operates. *We will take care of you.*

Getting to be a small part of that felt like a real honor. No design restrictions, some shiny paint, and the group's logo in vector. A joy from start to finish.
